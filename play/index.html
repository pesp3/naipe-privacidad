Naipe: Card Logic Puzzle

You are never dealt a hand. You deal the last card.

The table is set, every seat has its cards, and one slot is still empty. Your job is to choose which card goes there, and with that single choice, decide how the table ends.

No luck. One answer.

Nothing is shuffled, nothing is rolled, nothing is hidden behind a face-down card. Everything you need is on the table from the first second. Every level has exactly one card that meets the objective, and the wrong cards are the real puzzle: most candidates are traps.

What is inside

  • 951 levels, every one verified to have a single solution
  • Tables of 2, 3 or 4 seats, and five objectives
  • Hints that never give you the answer
  • A time attack mode, with a daily run that is the same for everyone
  • A four-colour deck you can turn on, for telling suits apart at a glance
